# Product Roadmap

## Phase 1: MVP

- Multi-tenant RBAC
- Customer and vendor master
- Product catalog with inventory type
- Quotation and sales order lifecycle
- Purchase invoice intake
- Core accounting postings
- Dashboard and reports

## Phase 2: Finance and compliance

- Auto-posting ledger engine
- GSTIN enrichment
- GSTR-1, 2B, 3B reports
- Excel and JSON export
- Audit trails

## Phase 3: Field ops

- Service ticket module
- AMC contract workflow
- Engineer allocation
- Site reports and repair status

## Phase 4: AI and automation

- OCR invoice intake
- Fallback AI extraction
- Auto-draft purchase invoice workflow
- Cashflow and profitability insights
